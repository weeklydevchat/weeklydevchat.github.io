## Join in two steps

<ol class="steps">
  <li><p><strong>Register on <a href="https://luma.com/weeklydevchat">Luma</a>.</strong> This is how you get the Zoom link, and you only have to do it once.</p></li>
  <li><p><strong>Open the link at 12pm Mountain Time</strong> on any Tuesday you feel like joining. No need to install Zoom &mdash; you can <a href="https://support.zoom.us/hc/en-us/articles/201362593-Join-My-Meeting-via-Browser">run it in your browser</a>.</p></li>
</ol>

## Already in one of these?

You only need one way in &mdash; pick whichever community you are already part of.

<section class="support two">
  <div class="support-card">
    <span class="card-eyebrow">Option</span>
    <h3>Dev Edmonton Slack</h3>
    <p>The link is posted every Tuesday in <code>#meetup-weekly-dev-chat</code>. It is also where topics get suggested and where the conversation keeps going afterwards. Free, and you don't have to live in Edmonton. <a href="https://devedmonton.com/#about-our-slack">Join the Slack</a></p>
  </div>
  <div class="support-card">

</section>

## What the hour is like

<section class="support plain">
  <div class="support-card">
    <h3>Cameras off is fine</h3>
    <p>Cameras and mics are both optional. Listening quietly is a completely normal way to attend, and plenty of people do.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="support-card">
    <h3>Nothing to prepare</h3>
    <p>A starter topic gets posted each week, but the conversation drifts wherever it wants to. You don't have to have read or done anything.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="support-card">
    <h3>Any experience level</h3>
    <p>Students, career switchers, and people twenty years in all end up in the same call. Be kind, supportive, and respectful of others &mdash; that's the whole rulebook.</p>
  </div>
</section>

## Common questions

<div class="qa">
  <div><p class="q">Do I have to be in Edmonton?</p><p class="a">No. The Tuesday chat is virtual and open to anyone, anywhere. Only the occasional in-person events are Edmonton-based.</p></div>
  <div><p class="q">Do I need to come every week?</p><p class="a">No. Show up when it works for you &mdash; a lot of people drop in every few weeks.</p></div>
  <div><p class="q">Is it recorded?</p><p class="a">No. Nothing is recorded or published, so people can speak freely.</p></div>
  <div><p class="q">What if I'm late?</p><p class="a">Join anyway. People come and go throughout the hour.</p></div>
  <div><p class="q">Can I suggest a topic?</p><p class="a">Yes. Post it in <code>#meetup-weekly-dev-chat</code> on Dev Edmonton Slack, or just raise it during the chat.</p></div>
</div>
