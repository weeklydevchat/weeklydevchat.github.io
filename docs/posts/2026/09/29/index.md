In our work, failure is no surprise. Builds break, tests fail, or something happens in production. We've all seen it. The question is, what do you do next? What have you learned from failures you've seen? Do you learn more from others or from your own mistakes?

What habits do you or your team use to learn from these failures? After something breaks, what do you actually change: the code, the tests, the process?
