Our first Show and Tell went so well that we're doing it again. Join us in person on **Thursday, November 19th** at the [Edmonton Public Library](https://www.epl.ca/) - [Strathcona](https://www.epl.ca/locations/eplstr/) to show what you've been building and see what other Edmonton developers are working on.

Last time we saw a 3D engine written from scratch in Vulkan, an optimized picoCTF solution, a deep dive into processes and threads, an AI chess tutor, and a game concept. This time we want even more of the real thing: **no slides, just code and prototypes.**

## The Format

Demos are 5–10 minutes, followed by a few minutes of questions. Open your editor, run your prototype, step through your terminal, or bring your hardware. Some ideas:

- A side project or product you're building, even if it's half-broken
- A piece of code you're proud of (or confused by)
- A bug you finally squashed or a problem you recently solved
- A CTF challenge walkthrough
- Something you learned and can demonstrate live
- A hardware or electronics project

Works-in-progress are welcome. We'll have a table, a podium, and a TV with HDMI, so bring your laptop and any adapters you need.

Planning to demo? Let us know when you register so we can plan the lineup.

Not presenting? Come anyway to watch, ask questions, and meet other devs. Light snacks and drinks are provided.

## Schedule

- 6:45pm – Doors open
- 7:00pm – Intro
- 7:10pm – Demos
- 8:30pm – Closing remarks and cleanup
